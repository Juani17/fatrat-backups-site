Privacy Policy

FatRat Backups is a private, owner-operated backup integration using rclone and Google Drive. It is not offered as a public service.

Google data accessed

When authorized by the account owner, rclone can access Google Drive files and related metadata as permitted by the OAuth scope selected by that owner. This access is used to upload, list, check, retrieve, and manage the owner's backup archives.

How the data is used and stored

Backup archives are intended to be encrypted locally before uploading to the owner's Google Drive. The OAuth authorization is maintained in a private rclone configuration on the owner's server, not in this public website. Backup files remain in the owner's Drive until the owner or the configured retention process removes them.

Sharing and tracking

FatRat Backups does not sell Google user data, serve ads, or use accessed Drive files for analytics. Google Drive stores files as the provider chosen by the owner. This informational website does not contain backup data, login forms, analytics scripts, or advertising scripts; it is hosted by GitHub Pages under GitHub's own service policies.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke Google account access through Google Account connections and can delete the uploaded backups from their Google Drive. Revoking access stops future authorized transfers; it does not automatically delete files already uploaded.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the project owner through GitHub.
